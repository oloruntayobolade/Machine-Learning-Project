# Machine-Learning-Project
This project is aimed to build a machine learning model to predict the aggregate rating of a restaurant based on features such as cuisines, price range and online delivery and others. Also to developed a machine learning model to classify restaurants based on their cuisines.

INSIGHTS: 
(1) The availability of Table booking is negatively correlated to the restaurant ratings, hence restaurants with table bookings tends to get lower ratings. 

(2) The availability of Online delivery is slightly positively correlated to the restaurants ratings, hence, restaurants with online delivery tends to get higher ratings.

(3) The availability of 24hours delivery (Is delivering now) is slightly positively correlated to the restaurants ratings, hence, those with this attribute tends to get higher ratings.

(4) The attribute; Switch to order menu is shown to have no correlation with the restaurants rating at all, hence have no significance to the ratings.

(5) The price range has the strongest positive correlation with the restaurants ratings. This implies that restaurants with wider price range have higher tendency of getting higher ratings. 

(6) From the top ten cities with thee restaurants having the best ratings, two cities, "Inner City and Mandaluyong City" are revealed to both belong to the top ten  with highest ratings and highest price range, which implies that cities with wider price range are more likely to get better ratings
